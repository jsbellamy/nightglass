The fusion holds: neck and head emerge from the crust lip with no gap or tabletop plane suggesting "duck + object." Quantized identity holds at 1×—crimped rim, crossing lattice, red gaps, slate tin lip, projecting bill, narrow red eye, tucked wing, and two webbed feet all remain legible in both the cohort and standalone bottom strip. Silhouette separation (C3) passes under a strict bar.
