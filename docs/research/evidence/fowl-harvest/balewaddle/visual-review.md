## Rationale

At native 1× the isolated Balewaddle below the cohort strip reads in the intended sequence: yellow duck head with oversized orange bill and single red eye, immediately followed by a rectangular straw torso bound with two dark twine bands, resolving as a squat, blocky farm mutant with a very low center of gravity. The duck is fused into the front face of the bale rather than perched on or peeking over it—the head plane and straw block share a continuous silhouette with no gap or layering cue. Compared to the cohort strip, Balewaddle is clearly distinct from the two tall vertical silhouettes (Cornquacker's husk column, Milkshake Mallard's tapered cup) and from Burger Drake's other squat form: Drake reads as a rounded burger mass with soft circular contour, while Balewaddle is broader, harder-edged, and horizontally dominant. All identity-critical pixels survive quantization at 1×—the bill, eye, both twine bands, shoulder/underside straw bristle, the yellow wing patch breaking the straw plane, and two complete orange webbed feet planted wide beneath the block.
