## Rationale

At native 1× the Mallard sprite resolves in the intended sequence: yellow duck head and oversized orange bill read first, then the smooth cream cylindrical cup torso with its horizontal red band, then the bent teal straw crest completing the diner-mutant silhouette. The spill collar reads as a distinct drooping cream plane between head and cup, not merged into either.

Silhouette separation is the critical bar and it clears. Against Burger Drake (top-left reference), Mallard is unmistakably tall and narrow versus squat and broad — no collision. Against Cornquacker (top-center reference), both are tall but Mallard's outline is smooth and cylindrical with a forward lean, while Cornquacker is irregular, jagged, and leafy; at 1× they read as two different tall Fowl archetypes, not variants of the same shape.

All identity features survive quantization as discrete pixel clusters. The single condiment-red eye, teal straw, red band, spill collar, small yellow wing, and two orange webbed feet are each legible without bleeding into adjacent planes. The feet emerge from the cup base as fused anatomy, and the head rises from the spill collar — the figure reads as one mutant body, not a duck placed inside or on a cup.
